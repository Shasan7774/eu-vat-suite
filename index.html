The problem

EU VAT breaks more WooCommerce stores than any other tax rule

Selling across 27 member states means 27 VAT rates, B2B exemptions, distance-selling thresholds and quarterly OSS returns. Get it wrong and you under-collect, over-collect, or fail an audit.

27 rates to get right

B2C buyers pay VAT at their country's rate — not yours. Hard-coding one rate quietly overcharges or undercharges most of your EU orders.

B2B reverse-charge

A valid EU business customer should pay zero VAT — but only if their VAT number checks out against VIES. Skip the check and you carry the liability.

OSS & reporting

Cross €10,000 in cross-border B2C sales and you owe destination VAT plus an OSS return. Reconstructing that from raw orders by hand is painful and error-prone.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Keystep EU VAT Suite actually do?

It makes your WooCommerce store charge the correct EU VAT on every order automatically — the right rate for B2C customers in each country, and zero VAT for valid cross-border B2B customers under the reverse-charge rules. Pro adds the reporting you need to file your OSS return and survive an audit.

Do I need the Pro version?

No — the free core plugin charges VAT correctly at checkout on its own. You only need Pro once you start crossing the €10,000 cross-border threshold and need OSS reporting, the threshold tracker, and audit export.

How does B2B reverse-charge work?

When a customer enters their EU VAT number at checkout, the plugin validates it against the EU's official VIES service. If it's valid and the sale is cross-border B2B, VAT is set to zero in line with the reverse-charge rules — all automatically.

What happens if the VIES service is down?

Your checkout never breaks. If VIES is slow or unavailable, the order falls back to charging standard VAT and is flagged for your review, so you never lose a sale and never silently exempt someone you shouldn't.

Does it conflict with my current tax settings?

No. Keystep EU VAT Suite is designed to coexist with your existing WooCommerce tax rates — it adds what's missing and leaves any rates you've already set up untouched.
